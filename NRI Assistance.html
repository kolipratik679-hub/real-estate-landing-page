👤 Who is an NRI?

An NRI (Non-Resident Indian) is an Indian citizen living abroad for work, business, or other purposes. NRIs can buy, sell, and invest in residential and commercial properties in India, with some restrictions on agricultural land, plantations, and farmhouses.
